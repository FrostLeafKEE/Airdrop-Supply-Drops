# Signal tube source material

- Tool: built-in image generation, one new raster atlas; no external image-edit workflow.
- Source: `signal-tube-atlas.png`, original 1254 × 1254 pixels. Four flat material quadrants.
- Export: `../build-signal-tube.py` crops and downsamples the original quadrants into game textures, creates the model JSON and embedded-texture Blockbench project. `../build-pbr.py` derives material data maps.
- Final geometry: **rectangular tube**, olive body, hollow square mouth, metal sleeve and five square grip ribs. The initial texture prompt mentioned an octagonal tube; it generated flat materials only, and the final mesh follows the user's later rectangular-tube instruction.

## Original generation prompt

Create a production Minecraft mod texture atlas for a slender single-use SIGNAL FLARE TUBE item inspired by an olive drab military handheld signal launcher: long olive painted tube, dull silver-gray metal lower sleeve and closely spaced screw/rib rings at the base, dark hollow mouth. This is SOURCE TEXTURE ART ONLY, no object rendering. Square 1024x1024 atlas with EXACT four equally-sized flat quadrants, no borders or gaps: TOP LEFT seamless worn olive-drab painted metal surface, subtle longitudinal grime/fibre streaks and small restrained edge chips, uniform base colour; TOP RIGHT seamless matte brushed silver-gray steel, fine horizontal machining scratches and weathering, no bolts or shapes; BOTTOM LEFT very dark charcoal interior material, softly mottled near-black, uniformly flat with no perspective; BOTTOM RIGHT olive paint of exactly same hue as upper-left, with a small dark black stencil rectangular label centred, legible uppercase text 'SIGNAL' and three tiny short horizontal marking lines underneath, aged stencil. Orthographic flat pixel-style hand-painted game material, crisp details, restrained wear, no strong baked directional shadows, no 3D objects, no gradients implying curvature, no mockup, no lighting, no transparency, no logos, no extra text, no weapons other than the generic signal tube materials. Upper two quadrants should tile naturally. Use authentic olive green and cold gray rather than gold. It will be sliced and downsampled to 128/64-pixel texture tiles and used on an octagonal tube model. Match the existing Minecraft aesthetic with enough detail for close inspection.
